**Style:** One bullet per entry, ideally ≤ 20 words, active voice, present tense verbs ("Add…", "Fix…", "Reduce…"). Expand cryptic internal shorthand into clear wording only when the entry's meaning is unambiguous; when unclear, preserve the original wording rather than interpreting it.
